### Verdict
Ship a simple public story: **MP5-L for lossless, MP5-C for future lossy distribution**. Remove C2 and H from normal export; neither currently delivers the user value required for a separate public format.

### Recommended product policy
- Public lineup:
  - **MP5-L** ù default, bit-exact lossless.
  - **MP5-C** ù lossy distribution, but remain lab-only until validated against MP3 at matched bitrates.
  - **PCM** ù advanced/reference only.
- Do not show C2 or H in landing-page format cards or normal Converter choices.
- Keep legacy C, C2, and H decodable with accurate diagnostic labels.
- Never present classic MP5-C as the future distribution codec: its scalar quantizer is structurally unsuitable.
- Internally, C may use lossless escape frames for silence, tails, or fragile transients. Public copy can call this ôadaptive perceptual coding,ö but technical documentation must disclose the lossless fallback.

### Technical path for C
- Base the successor on the **MDCT path**, not classic MP5-C scalar quantization.
- Retain useful C2 research:
  - Quiet/tail detection and hysteresis.
  - Per-band fragility detection.
  - Coalescing and MP5-L escape units.
- Replace the current simplistic MDCT allocation with:
  - Proper masking thresholds and scalefactor bands.
  - Tonal-versus-noise analysis.
  - Temporal masking and robust pre-echo control.
  - Stereo coupling decisions.
  - VBR/target-bitrate rate control and, likely, a bit reservoir.
  - Better coefficient entropy coding.
- Establish bitrate ladders and compare at matched output sizes. Current reference points:
  - MP3 320 kbps: **0.227╫ PCM** on the two-track 16-bit corpus.
  - MDCT High: **0.214╫ PCM** on one real track, roughly the same bitrate class.
  - MDCT Extreme: **0.268╫ PCM**.
- Require a held-out, multi-genre corpus including speech, tonal signals, cymbals, transients, stereo ambience, fades, and known codec ôkiller samples.ö
- Run blinded ABX/MUSHRA against source and LAME MP3 at matched bitrates. ôLow hiss riskö is not equivalent to perceptual transparency.
- Suggested release gate:
  - No statistically demonstrated inferiority to the selected MP3 anchor at matched size.
  - No systematic pre-echo, warbling, stereo collapse, clipping, duration drift, or quiet-tail hiss.
  - Multiple independent listeners and at least 20û30 held-out tracks.
  - Deterministic encode/decode, bounded malformed-input handling, fuzzing, streaming/seek validation, and browser performance coverage.
- Do not claim ôbeats MP3ö unless a preregistered, reproducible comparison supports both quality and size. ôMP3-class experimental lossy codecö is the strongest defensible interim wording.

### What to do with H
**Pick B+, remove user export but retain decode and lab encoding.**

Why:
- ORIGAMI MP5-H High is **1.54╫ PCM and 2.01╫ MP5-L** despite being bit-exact.
- C2-as-base H is approximately **1.00╫ MP5-L**, but still not smaller than L.
- The Converter already size-gates H and commonly resolves an H request to MP5-L, which is technically honest but product-confusing.
- Its fallback base is classic MP5-C, whose audible hiss undermines the main layered-format benefit.

Policy:
- Keep `CodecId.MP5H = 3` and enhanced/base-only decode indefinitely.
- Preserve missing-CORR warnings.
- Keep encoder and benchmarks available through lab/CLI paths.
- Reconsider visible export only if H demonstrates a real workflow advantage beyond ôlarger lossless file with a degraded fallback.ö

### What to do with C2
- Retire **MP5-C2** as a public product name.
- Remove its first-class Converter option and public landing/About cards.
- Preserve `CodecId.MP5C2 = 5`, magic `0x43 0x34`, and decoding for existing files.
- Label existing files in diagnostics as something like **ôLegacy MP5-C2 adaptive/lossless-restore profile.ö**
- Do not repurpose CodecId 5 as the final non-bit-exact MP5-C without an explicit wire-version/profile distinction.
- Prefer a new frozen codec/profile identifier for the eventual shipping C bitstream.
- Keep C2Æs protection and framing ideas as research inputs, not as another marketed format.
- Keep classic `CodecId.MP5C = 1` decode-compatible and lab-only; do not silently redefine it.

### Risks & disagreements with the floated collapse plan
- I agree with collapsing the public story, but disagree with simply relabeling current C2 as C:
  - Current Rust C2 encoding chooses `min(bit-exact SR+CORR, MP5-L)` for loud units.
  - That makes the current shipping path effectively **bit-exact**, not the requested lossy distribution lane.
- Documentation is materially out of sync:
  - Some docs describe C2 as lossy loud-path content with optional correction.
  - Current code always constructs correction for the modern SR path.
  - The committed real-track JSON still reports `contentBitExact: false`.
- The existing C2 ABX protocol is inappropriate for a genuinely bit-exact output; sample equality should be mechanically verified. ABX belongs on the lossy MDCT successor.
- Calling C ônot hybridö is acceptable product simplification, but technical material must still disclose lossless escape frames if used.
- Current MDCT evidence proves promising **size**, not MP3-equivalent quality. Tail preservation plus 26.8 dB full SNR does not measure pre-echo, tonal distortion, stereo image, or transient smearing.
- Classic CÆs full-song SNR hides catastrophic quiet-window behavior. It must not survive as the foundation merely to preserve naming continuity.
- Removing H export sacrifices a research workflow, but that workflow has no demonstrated end-user value sufficient to justify public format complexity.
