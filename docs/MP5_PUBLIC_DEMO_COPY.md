# MP5 Public Demo Copy

**Version:** MP5 Audio v0.28.0-beta  
**Badge:** `MP5 Public Beta - v0.28.0-beta`

## Short Description

MP5 Audio is an experimental browser-based audio format, converter, and player. MP5-L v4 is the recommended lossless path; MP5-C, MP5-C2 (vNext), and MP5-H are lab/research modes (not default).

Public Beta also includes metadata/cover/lyrics, VISU themes, a local library on this device, and experimental user-provided stems (including batch stem import and karaoke when stems + synced lyrics are present). Album packages (`.mp5p`) are experimental. There is no AI stem separation.

## Honesty Copy

MP5 is Public Beta and not production-ready for archival or legal use. It does not claim to beat MP3, AAC, Opus, or FLAC. It does not enforce DRM or provide rights/legal verification.

## Privacy Copy

The reference app processes audio locally in the browser. It does not add telemetry, upload, or cloud sync. Local library data stays in browser storage on this device.

## Demo Fixture Copy

Hosted demos use synthetic audio fixtures only. Users may open or convert their own files locally, but copyrighted/private local audio must not be committed or deployed.
